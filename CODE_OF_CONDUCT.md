# Code of Conduct

Imagine-Reader follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone taking part in the project is expected to follow it. This includes
issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

In short:

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Welcome people of every background and level of experience.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, never on the person.
- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, and sexual language or imagery are
  not acceptable.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through
the contact details on [@ihsonnet's GitHub profile](https://github.com/ihsonnet).
You can also use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue, or
pull request. Every report is read and kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, and issues that break
this code, and may ban anyone from the project for repeated or serious
behaviour, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
